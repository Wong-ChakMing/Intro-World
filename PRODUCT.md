## Platform

web

## Stack

Vite + three.js (npm), vanilla JavaScript modules.

## Users

Two audiences, weighted equally:

- Recruiters and hiring managers evaluating Wong Chak Ming for software / AIoT / embedded roles. They need experience, skills and contact details within seconds.
- Developers and friends who enjoy the playful Minecraft world and the stick-man guide.

## Product Purpose

A personal introduction site for Wong Chak Ming. The visitor explores a small Minecraft-style voxel world; a stick-man guide walks to whichever chest the visitor picks, opens it, and the CV content is shown inside the chest's inventory screen.

## Positioning

The CV is presented as a world the visitor plays in rather than a page they scroll. The owner builds real-time IoT systems and lists Three.js and Unity among his skills, so the site itself is a working demonstration of that.

## Operating Context

Opened from a CV link, LinkedIn profile or GitHub profile, on desktop or phone. Must work with mouse, touch and keyboard.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Five separate chests, each a different chest type: About, Experience, Projects, Skills, Contact.
- Clicking a chest (or choosing it from the hotbar) sends the stick man to it; he opens it and the content appears in the chest screen.
- All content must also be reachable without the 3D scene (hotbar, keyboard, whole-CV view).
- Static hosting only; no backend.

## Brand Commitments

- Minecraft-styled visual world (pinned by the owner).
- A stick-man guide character.
